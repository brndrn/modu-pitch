MODU is the pioneering Swiss consumer health brand transferring the explosive global growth of the **modern white oral pouch** (the delivery format powering ZYN and VELO at >35% CAGR) into **100% tobacco-free, nicotine-free functional wellness**.

### Slide 03: The Problem with Traditional Delivery Systems
* **Header:** `02 // THE PROBLEM` | `TRADITIONAL SUPPLEMENT LIMITATIONS`
* **Title:** Kapseln und Tabletten sind im Jahr 2026 überholt.
* **Three Critical Flaws:**
  1. **Träge Resorption:** Capsules require 30 to 60 minutes for gastric breakdown. Unsuitable for acute cravings, energy slumps, or sudden travel nausea.
  2. **First-Pass-Leberabbau:** Up to 60% of delicate botanical actives are destroyed by stomach acid and hepatic first-pass metabolism before entering systemic circulation.
  3. **Unpraktisch & Klebrig:** Requires water to swallow. Gummies are packed with sugar, glucose syrup, and stick to teeth. Powders require messy shakers and cause bloating.
* **Speaker Notes:** "When you suffer from motion sickness on an alpine mountain pass or a sugar craving at 3 PM, you don't have 45 minutes to wait for a pill to dissolve in your stomach. You need bioavailability in under 5 minutes."

---

### Slide 04: The MODU Solution (Transmucosal Bioavailability)
* **Header:** `03 // THE SOLUTION` | `TRANSMUCOSAL BIOAVAILABILITY`
* **Title:** Mundschleimhaut statt Magen-Darm-Passage.
* **Biological Mechanism:** The oral buccal pouch rests comfortably between the upper gum and lip. The non-keratinized epithelial lining absorbs lipid- and water-soluble active molecules directly into the facial venous plexus and jugular vein.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * **Active in <5 Minutes:** Immediate physiological uptake.
  * **100% Bypass of Liver First-Pass:** Superior bioavailability with lower therapeutic doses.
  * **Complete Mobility:** Zero water needed, zero chewing, zero calories, zero dental decay.
* **Benchmark Chart:** Pouch buccal uptake (3–5 min) vs Chewing Gum/Shots (15–25 min) vs Standard Capsules (45–60 min).
* **Speaker Notes:** "By utilizing transmucosal diffusion, MODU achieves a pharmaceutical-grade absorption curve without requiring a prescription or an invasive delivery method."
